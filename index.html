      Visas & entry requirements

      🇰🇪

      Kenya

      • Most visitors apply online for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) before travel — allow at least 3 working days.
      • Passport valid 6+ months with blank pages.
      • Yellow fever vaccination recommended; certificate needed if arriving from a risk country.
      🇹🇿

      Tanzania

      • Apply for an e-Visa via the official Tanzania Immigration portal before travel.
      • Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from (or transiting 12+ hours in) a risk country — including Kenya.
      🇺🇬

      Uganda

      • Apply for an e-Visa online, or the East Africa Tourist Visa (Kenya · Uganda · Rwanda).
      • Yellow fever certificate required for all travellers aged 1+.
      • Gorilla permits (US$800) are included in our Uganda tours.
      Entry rules change frequently. Always confirm on the official government website for your nationality before booking flights. Use only official portals — never pay third-party "visa agents".
